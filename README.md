Here’s a professional and polite draft for your maternity leave and work-from-home request email:

⸻

Subject: Maternity Leave and Work-from-Home Request

Dear [Manager’s Name],

I hope you are doing well.

I would like to formally inform you that I am planning to go on maternity leave starting from the end of June. In light of this, I also wanted to request the possibility of working from home starting immediately or as early as feasible. Due to my current health condition, commuting to the office daily has become increasingly difficult.

I assure you that my productivity and commitment to work will remain unaffected, and I will continue to fulfill all my responsibilities and deadlines while working from home.

Please let me know if any formal procedures need to be followed or if you require any documentation from my end. I appreciate your support and understanding during this time.

Warm regards,
[Your Full Name]
[Your Designation]
[Employee ID, if applicable]
